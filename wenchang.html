For our international exchange students, welcome to Taiwan! Adapting to a new country and academic life can sometimes be challenging and overwhelming.

In traditional Chinese culture, there is a profound concept known as 'Wen Chang' (文昌), which symbolizes wisdom, focus, and inner clarity. We warmly invite you to join us for a unique Mindfulness & Cultural Experience, where you can immerse yourself in ancient traditions, relax your mind, and find inner peace amid your busy student life.

什麼是文昌？ What Is Wen Chang?

相信大家都有經驗，大腦卡關、心很浮躁的時候，明明坐在書桌前三小時，公式怎麼算都算不通。

在我們中華傳統文化裡，有一個專門掌管「學業」的正能量，叫做「文昌」。大家常聽到的「文昌祈福」，最核心的目的不是求運氣，而是幫我們的心平靜下來，開啟專注潛能。

我們社團接下來會舉辦文昌祈福，打開我們的潛能，用更有效的方式提升專注力，上台報告前也能減少緊張感。

You have probably been there: your mind feels stuck and restless, you have been at your desk for three hours, and the problem still will not work out.

Traditional Chinese culture has a positive force devoted to learning, called Wen Chang. A Wen Chang blessing is not really about asking for luck. At its heart, it is about calming the mind and unlocking your ability to focus.

Our club is holding a Wen Chang blessing to help you tap into that potential, build focus more effectively, and feel less nervous before presentations.

活動當天 On the Day

活動當天除了會讓大家寫文昌祈福卡之外，我們還會有以下文化體驗： Besides writing your own Wen Chang blessing card, you will take part in:

  1. 🥬

    吃素一天A Day of Vegetarian Eating

    當天體驗齋戒吃素，幫身體做個淨化，晚上就用最乾淨、輕鬆的狀態來感受當下的平靜。 Eat vegetarian for the day as a light cleanse, so that by evening you can take in the calm feeling clear and relaxed.

  2. 🙏

    古禮體驗Traditional Ceremony

    現場會帶領大家體驗傳承了兩千多年、如同祭孔大典般莊嚴的古禮。大家平常生活和讀書步調快、心中很雜，透過古禮的沉靜動作，能讓自己慢下來，體驗一種古代讀書人極致的專注與謙卑。 We will guide you through a ceremony handed down for more than two thousand years, as solemn as the rites honouring Confucius. Daily life and study move fast and the mind gets crowded; the ceremony's quiet, deliberate movements help you slow down and feel the deep focus and humility of scholars of old.

  3. 📜

    功德費Offering Fee

    用來登記「智慧名冊」跟準備當天的水果供品，一生只要一次，代表對自己開啟這趟智慧體驗的重視。 Covers your entry in the "Wisdom Register" and the fruit offerings for the day. It is needed only once in a lifetime, and marks your commitment to beginning this journey.

大家要在大學面對期中考，這場非常有質感的文化體驗，能幫大家的心定下來、加持學業，真的蠻建議大家可以去體驗看看！ With midterms coming up, this thoughtful cultural experience can help settle your mind and support your studies. We really recommend giving it a try!
